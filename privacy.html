Privacy Policy

How we protect member data

IronTemple respects the privacy of every member. Personal data is used to manage memberships, schedule services, and support wellness planning.

We collect standard account and fitness information such as email, phone number, membership details, and progress-related preferences when voluntarily provided.

This information is stored securely and used only for operational, communication, and service-improvement purposes. It is not sold to third parties.

Members may request access to their stored profile details or ask for corrections to ensure accurate records. We may use cookies or similar tools to enhance website functionality and analytics.

Any future changes to this policy will be communicated through visible website notices or direct member communication.
